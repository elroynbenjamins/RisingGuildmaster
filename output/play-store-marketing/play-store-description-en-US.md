# App name

Rising Guildmaster

# Short description

Recruit heroes, build your guild, and command tactical battles across Eldoria.

# Full description

Three recruits. One contract. Who earns a place in your guild?

Take command in Rising Guildmaster, a fantasy guild-management RPG with detailed pixel art and tactical turn-based battles. Assemble your company, prepare for each expedition, and guide your adventurers through the world of Eldoria.

RECRUIT YOUR COMPANY
Meet adventurers from different races and classes. Weigh their abilities, traits, potential, hiring fees, and weekly salaries. Choose the heroes who suit your party and your treasury, then train and equip them for the road ahead.

MAKE EVERY TURN COUNT
Position your heroes on tactical battlefields and choose the right ability for the moment. Use ranged attacks, spells, healing, and support skills to face dangerous enemies and raid bosses. Develop your heroes' skill paths as they level up, from an archer's opening shot to a volley of arrows.

EXPLORE ELDORIA
Travel through five distinct regions, discover settlements, take on quests, and follow the story campaign. Prepare your supplies and party before setting out on your next adventure.

CHOOSE YOUR DUNGEON ROUTE
Venture into branching dungeons with battles, elite enemies, events, treasure, and opportunities to recover. Decide when to press on and how to prepare for the boss waiting deeper below.

LEAD FROM THE WAR TABLE
Keep your guild ready for its next expedition. Manage resources, upgrade facilities, craft equipment, brew potions, and care for injured heroes. Balance today's needs with your plans for a stronger company.

DEVELOP YOUR HEROES
Build a varied roster, unlock abilities, and shape your party around the challenges ahead. Choose Standard, Veteran, or Iron Guild difficulty to match the challenge you want.

Gather your heroes. Choose your next move. Build your guild's legacy.
